# Rights and stewardship policy

Marginalia is an index first and a file archive second.

## Publication rules

1. Metadata, short editorial summaries, and canonical links may be indexed when
   their source terms allow it.
2. Files may be mirrored only under an explicit redistribution license, clear
   public-domain status, or documented permission from the rights holder.
3. Open access, free download, public GitHub visibility, and an accessible URL
   are not sufficient evidence by themselves.
4. Attribution, license links, notices, noncommercial restrictions, no-
   derivatives restrictions, and share-alike obligations must travel with the
   record and file.
5. A document with conflicting notices or uncertain version-level rights stays
   link-only or in review.

## Minimum evidence for a mirror

- Exact source URL and retrieval date
- SHA-256 digest of the reviewed file
- License name and canonical license URL
- Location of the document-level rights notice
- Authors, publisher, and required attribution statement
- Reviewer identity and decision timestamp

## Corrections and takedowns

Every public record should expose a correction and takedown route. A challenged
mirror should be disabled while its rights record is reviewed; metadata can
remain when lawful and useful, with a note explaining the change.
