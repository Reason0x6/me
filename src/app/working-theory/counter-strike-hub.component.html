02 / Desk rules

A result is where the analysis starts.

  1. 01
    Declare the window

    Every form claim names its dates, event set or roster boundary.

  2. 02
    Keep the denominator

    A 100% record over six maps is reported as six maps, not invincibility.

  3. 03
    Separate levels

    Rounds, maps, series and trophies answer different competitive questions.

  4. 04
    Leave a falsification test

    Strong conclusions state what future result would make them change.
