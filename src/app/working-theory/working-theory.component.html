04 / About this place

A workshop for ideas, not a showroom for certainty.

Working Theory is a personal publication by Gavin Austin. It exists to give unfinished thoughts enough structure to become discussable—and enough history to be corrected later.

Nothing here is presented as timeless instruction. Dates, revisions, related notes, and sources are part of the writing because changing your mind should leave a useful trace.
