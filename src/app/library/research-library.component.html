04 / Rights & stewardship

Publicly reachable is not the same as public domain.

Marginalia is an index first. A document is copied into archive storage only when its license, public-domain status, or author permission clearly allows redistribution. Everything else keeps its canonical source link.

ReusableLicense evidence recorded. Eligible for a faithful attributed mirror.
Link onlyDiscoverable here; the document remains with its original publisher.
Rights reviewUseful metadata found, but ownership or reuse terms need a human decision.
