03 / FROM FILE TO WORKBENCH

From the first layer.

Fit samples first. Then a case, its matching carriers, and your chosen mount.

01 Print and tune the fit samples

Start with unfilled PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers. Use enough perimeters to make thin clips solid. These are prototype starting settings, not a tested printer profile.

  1. Print ClipBase.stl and Shoe_0p45.stl. Compare the 0.35 and 0.55 mm variants if needed.
  2. Lower the shoe onto its locating pins and support pads. Its windows should latch under the clip hooks. Push the clips outward to release.
  3. Print both lid samples and follow the orientation guide below.
  4. For Gridfinity, print the foot and socket samples and check your actual baseplate.

Remove strings and burrs from mating faces. Larger shoe clearance reduces hook engagement: keep ClipGap below ClipHook. Change dimensions in FreeCAD rather than scaling the STL.

02 Orient and assemble the lid tests
  1. Keep LidChannelCoupon upright, with its groove facing inward toward the lid piece.
  2. Flip LidLatchCoupon over from its printing orientation. The smooth face that touched the bed faces up; the raised tongue faces down.
  3. Align the tongue with the open rear end of the channel. Keep the flexible release tab at that rear end.
  4. Slide lengthways toward the front until the latch bump engages its pocket. The tongue enters from the end, not from above.
  5. To release, press the spring away from the channel, toward the lid centre, while sliding backward.

If it jams before the bump reaches the pocket, check orientation, stringing and sliding clearance. Do not force the tongue down through the top of the groove.

03 Print and assemble the enclosure
  1. Choose a case folder and print its base, front panel, matching carriers and one lid: Lid.stl or LidClosed.stl.
  2. STLs are oriented with base and carriers bottom-down, lid outside-face-down and panel on its broad face. Inspect grooves, clip ledges and bridges in your slicer; add local support where needed.
  3. Install the mounting adapter before the carriers, using the four screws through the inside floor. For SKÅDIS, attach the adapter to the board first.
  4. Attach electronics to loose carriers. C6 holders have trial M2 pilots; choose screw lengths for your PCB. WAGO carriers retain connectors using small cable ties.
  5. Lower carriers over the locating pins and press until the clips engage. Fit the removable front panel into its guide.
  6. Check connector insertion and wire routing, then feed the lid tongues from the rear and slide forward to latch.

Leave approximately one case depth clear behind the mounted box to remove the lid. Press the rear-right release pad inward and slide back. Release carrier clips outward with a small blunt tool before lifting; do not pull against the hooks.

04 Match your electronics and connection panel

The FireBeetle holder targets the FireBeetle 2 ESP32-C6 / DFR1075, with a 60 × 25.4 mm board outline and 56.6 × 22 mm mounting pitch. It needs a front-row 1×2 footprint.

The 14 × 8 mm rounded USB opening provides cable access to the recessed onboard USB-C socket. It is not a circular hole or a USB bulkhead. Socket offsets and cable overmould clearances still need checking with your actual board and cable.

The Ethernet example uses a 16.6 × 14.6 mm aperture and 3.8 mm bores on 25 mm centres, based on the Adafruit 909 example. The grommet bore is 12 mm. Connector bodies and cable bend radii may require moving a carrier or increasing the case height.

WAGO holders cover 221-412, 221-413 and 221-415. Other 221 families have different dimensions. Check lever travel and wire entry. The Grid 4×4 demonstrator has Ethernet and grommet openings but no C6 USB opening.

05 Customise the model in FreeCAD
  1. Download the complete source package. Open a case’s Enclosure.FCStd and edit column C in the Parameters spreadsheet, then recompute.
  2. For a new carrier arrangement, edit Layouts.json. Set cols, rows, cavity height, vents and module footprints.
  3. Use zero-based x/y cell positions and nx/ny footprint sizes. Avoid overlaps and keep modules inside the grid.
  4. Run GenerateSnapGrid.FCMacro alongside the JSON file to regenerate the matching cases and carriers. Save personal edits separately; generators replace output files.

Module kinds: blank, universal, c6, wago412, wago413, wago415 and cable. Vent choices: paired, curved or none.

Default clip gap is 0.45 mm per side; lid gap is 0.35 mm. Keep the inner-size formulas and at least 36 mm pitch for supplied hardware carriers. Spreadsheet changes do not add module topology, and native edits do not automatically update STLs.

Use GenerateMounts.FCMacro to rebuild adapters. Change Gridfinity foot counts in its generator build call; changing spreadsheet perimeter values alone does not add feet. Preserve the 42 mm mating grid and fixed case mounting pitches.
